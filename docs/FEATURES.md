## Plans

Free / Pro / Premium, defined in `src/modules/plans/catalogue.ts`.
**No payments exist** — paid tiers say "coming soon" everywhere.

| | Free | Pro | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core game (quests, character, skills, journal, challenges, community) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Active goals / projects | 3 / 2 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Experiments at a time | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| Journey history | 30 days | All time | All time |
| Insights | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| AI quests, roadmaps, summaries, insight reports, assessment feedback | — | — | ✓ |
| AI Mentor | — | — | ✓ (coming soon) |

- Limits are enforced in services (goal create/reopen/restore, project
  create/reopen, experiment start) with messages that say what to do next.
- Locked pages show what's inside and link to `/plans`; nothing is deleted
  when someone is over a limit.
- `/plans` records "notify me" interest (`PlanInterest`).
- Grant tiers by hand: `npm run plan:set -- friend@example.com pro`.
- AI runs only for Premium (`aiAllowedFor`), and only when an AI provider is
  configured.

## How each system works

### Daily quests (`modules/quests`, `quest.service.ts`)
- **One day, generated once** — on the first visit of the user's calendar
  day (their timezone), never regenerated by refreshes.
- **Five core slots**: Physical, Mental, Learning, Social, Personal Goal,
  plus an optional side quest for a creativity/experiences focus. The goal
  slot follows the major goal's next mission.
- Generation is seeded by user + date + slot, so it's reproducible.
  Generators (rule-based now, AI for Premium) share one contract; XP is
  always priced by the backend.
- **Flexible, never punitive** (spec §55): energy check-ins regenerate
  untouched quests; skipping asks what got in the way; "Tomorrow" carries a
  quest over once; unfinished quests simply expire.
- Learning quests can include a micro-lesson from the bundled library.

### Goals and roadmaps (`modules/goals`, `goal.service.ts`)
- Goal → milestones → missions. Progress is derived (completed ÷
  non-skipped missions), never typed in.
- Every roadmap generator returns the same validated draft; each one is
  saved as a `RoadmapRevision`. Milestones record who wrote them (user,
  template, AI).
- Goals are archived, never deleted from the UI.

### Progression (`server/progression/engine.ts`)
- Quest completed → validated → XP transaction → level and attributes
  recalculated. Never double-pays; invalid amounts are refused.
- Attributes follow life areas by fixed weights — progress indicators, not
  measurements of a person.

### Skills (`modules/skills`, `skill.service.ts`)
- 11 trees, synced from code into the database. Skills are deliberately
  unequal: each has its own XP cost, depth and unlock gate.
- A completed quest is routed to a skill by quest type → goal → topic →
  interests → life area; a locked target pays into its requirement chain.
- A top-level skill becomes **Mastered** only after a passed practical task
  or project review.

### Achievements, streaks, challenges, Shadow Debt
- **Achievements** are pure rules over real counts, checked after
  completions; paid once, never revoked.
- **Streaks** are derived from completion dates. Lifetime days and best run
  are permanent and lead the UI.
- **Challenges** are earned through matching quests (one credit per day),
  with one grace day per week; two at a time.
- **Shadow Debt** is a mirror, not a penalty: it counts core quests that
  expired unacknowledged in the last 14 days and suggests a lighter day. It
  never touches XP.

### Projects, assessments, Skill Passport
- Projects complete only with evidence and at least one skill; completion
  pays character XP and XP into every skill used. Reopening takes it back.
- **Quizzes** are graded on the server against a key the page never sees.
  **Practical tasks** and **project reviews** are scored on a weighted
  rubric — by AI (Premium) or the user's own ratings with evidence. Every
  result says who evaluated it. Only a first pass pays.
- The **Skill Passport** shows each skill's proof tier — Practiced, Tested,
  Demonstrated, Mastered — with the evidence behind it.

### Journal, reflection, timeline, experiments
- The **journal** is private: never on the passport, timeline, community,
  or in anything sent to AI.
- **End-of-day reflection**: five optional questions and a mood, editable
  for 7 days; writes a summary and one idea for tomorrow (AI for Premium,
  otherwise built from the user's own words). A rough day makes the next
  day gentler.
- The **life timeline** is derived from records and replayed ledgers.
- **Experiments** add one short practice quest per day for their window;
  the review asks how it went and can turn it into a goal.

### Journey and Insights
- **Journey** (`/journey`): level and tier path, weekly rings, XP chart,
  quest activity grid, consistency heatmap, life areas and energy map, goal
  and skill paths, weekly/monthly/yearly reviews, achievements. Free shows
  7 and 30 days; longer ranges are Pro.
- **Insights** (Pro): counts, not scores — quests per week and life area,
  XP, skill levels, goal momentum, challenges. **Patterns carry their
  evidence** and never claim a cause. With AI, the model may only rephrase
  real patterns; anything ungrounded is dropped.

### Community
- Only real members who chose to be visible appear; hidden fields are
  hidden, never zero. Private members are counted but never named. No feed.
- Guilds, group challenges (each person runs their own challenge window),
  opt-in leaderboards that lead with "you vs. your previous self", and
  suggestions that explain why.
- **Mentorship is two-sided**: pending until accepted; then private notes
  (rate-limited) until either ends it. Standing comes from real skill
  levels (3 to mentor, 7 for expert).

### Accounts, look and navigation
- Email + password and Google sign-in; self-service account deletion
  (see [AUTH_AND_SECURITY.md](AUTH_AND_SECURITY.md)).
- Black-and-white interface with colour reserved for elements — icons, life
  areas, badges, XP, progress (see [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)).
- Grouped sidebar, phone tab bar, instant loading states, installable as a
  home-screen app, link previews.
